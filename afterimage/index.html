Afterimage.io

An Android arcade survival game. Everything that hunts you is your own past.

Coming soon to Google Play

How it plays

You move through a fixed arena while Replicas replay the exact path you took earlier. They do not chase you; they follow where you have already been. Hunters replay your path faster. Ride a Slipstream to gain speed and put distance between you and what is behind you. Survive as long as you can.

Modes

  • Survival. The base mode: stay alive against Replicas and Hunters and set your best time.
  • Expert Mode. A harder version of survival.
  • Arcade Mode. Pick up Powerups and play through Nightdrive.
  • Hero Mode. Dungeon runs with three classes, skill trees and Masters (Boss) to defeat.
  • Afterimage Online. An arena match against bots for now.

Price

Free with ads. Premium removes ads. Optional shard packs are available as in-app purchases.
