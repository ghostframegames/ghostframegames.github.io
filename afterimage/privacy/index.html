Privacy Policy — Afterimage.io

Effective date: October 15, 2026
Last updated: November 23, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what information the mobile game Afterimage.io (package name com.afterimage.game, the "App") collects, why, who it is shared with, and what choices you have.

The App is published by Alec Vautherot, an individual developer trading as Ghostframe Games, of United States ("we", "us", "our"). For the purposes of the UK GDPR and EU GDPR we are the data controller for the processing described here, except where this policy states that Google acts as an independent controller.

If you do not agree with this policy, please do not install or use the App.

1. Summary
Do we require an account? No sign-up, ever. The App is fully playable with no sign-in and with no internet connection. When the App first posts one of your scores to the world rankings, or when you open Afterimage Online, it sets up an anonymous game account on our server: a random number and a random six-character tag, with no email, no password and no link to your Google account (§ 2.8).
Is the App free? Yes, with ads. It shows ads unless you buy the premium unlock, which removes them.
Do we run our own servers? Two, both hosted on Cloudflare. (1) World rankings and Afterimage Online: your anonymous game account, the display name you choose (optional), your ranked scores, and live matches against other players (§ 2.4, § 2.8). (2) Anonymous play statistics: if "Share anonymous play stats" is on (it is on by default, except in the EEA and the UK, where it starts off), the App sends a small record of how the game is played, which we use to improve the game's performance and to plan future improvements. It carries a random number made by the App, never your name or any account or advertising identifier. You can turn it off in Settings > Privacy (§ 2.7). We store no email addresses, no passwords and no game saves.
Where does your progress live? On your device. Best times, shards, unlocked cosmetics, achievements, hero and arcade progress, purchases and settings are stored in local app storage. Android may also back that storage up to your own Google account (§ 2.5). Your ranked scores are also kept on our rankings server (§ 2.8).
What leaves the device? Advertising identifier, approximate location, ad interaction data and diagnostics (to Google AdMob); your Play Games display name, player ID and submitted scores only if you sign in to Play Games; purchase transactions processed by Google Play Billing; your anonymous game account, optional display name and ranked scores, and your moves during a match against other players (§ 2.4, § 2.8); and, if "Share anonymous play stats" is on, anonymous play statistics (§ 2.7; on by default, except in the EEA and the UK, where it starts off).
Can other players see anything about you? Only what is on the rankings and in a match: your display name (or "Runner" if you chose none), your tag, your scores, and, during a match against other players, your unit, its look and its level (§ 2.4, § 2.8). There is no chat.
Do we sell your data? No. We never sell personal information for money. See § 9 for how US state privacy laws treat personalised advertising as "sharing".
Is the App for children? No. It is not directed to children under 13 and is rated for ages 13 and over. See § 8.

2. Information the App collects

2.1 Advertising: AdMob (rewarded and interstitial ads)

The App is funded by advertising supplied by Google AdMob. There are two kinds:

  • Rewarded ads. You choose whether to watch one; they are never forced. Watching one multiplies the shards earned from a run.
  • Interstitial ads. A full-screen ad that can appear between runs, on a schedule limited by number of runs and by time: not in your first session or first runs, and at most once per few minutes of play. It never appears during a run or on launch.

Buying the premium unlock turns both off. With it, the App stops requesting ads.

To serve, cap, measure and bill for ads, the Google Mobile Ads SDK collects and transmits to Google:

  • your device's Android Advertising ID (AAID) — a resettable identifier not tied to your name;
  • approximate location, derived from your IP address at the country/region level. The App does not request GPS or precise location, and no location permission appears in its manifest;
  • app interactions: which ad was requested, whether it was shown, whether it was watched to completion, whether it was clicked, and timestamps;
  • diagnostics: crash and performance data from the ad SDK, and technical data about the device and connection — device model, operating system version, app version, language, network type and screen size.

The legal basis for personalised advertising in the UK and EEA is your consent, collected through Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP), a Google-certified consent management platform, the first time an ad is available. Where you decline, the SDK requests non-personalised advertising, which still uses the advertising ID for frequency capping, fraud prevention and billing. In regions where the consent form is required you can review or change your choice at any time (§ 9.2).

Google acts as an independent controller for the advertising data it receives. Its own policy governs what it does with it: see § 6.

2.2 Google Play Games Services (optional) and leaderboards

The App integrates Google Play Games Services so scores can also appear on Google's leaderboards and achievements can be recorded against your Play Games profile. This is entirely optional — the App is fully functional signed out. The world rankings shown inside the App are run by us, not by Google, and do not need Play Games (§ 2.8); Play Games is a second, optional copy of your scores.

If you are signed in to Play Games, the following is processed:

  • your Play Games player ID and Play Games display name (a gamertag you chose in the Play Games app — not necessarily your real name);
  • your avatar image, as displayed by Google's own leaderboard interface;
  • the scores you set, submitted to the App's Play Games leaderboards: Best Survival Time and Weekly Best Time (Endless mode), Style score, Arcade score, and the Waves layer and Journey boards. When you sign in, your saved bests on these boards are submitted again so they appear;
  • achievement unlocks and progress, reported to Play Games as you earn them.

Leaderboards are public within Play Games: other players can see your display name, avatar and submitted scores. We do not receive, store or transmit your Google account email address, and we never see your Google password.

You control your Play Games visibility and can delete your Play Games profile and its data from the Google Play Games app itself — see § 10.

2.3 Purchases

Optional in-app purchases (shard packs, a premium unlock that removes ads, cosmetic and character packs) are processed entirely by Google Play Billing. Google, not us, handles your payment method, billing address, tax and receipts. We never see or receive your card details.

The App receives a purchase token from Google confirming a transaction succeeded. That token is stored in local app storage on your device, so the App knows what you own and never grants the same purchase twice. It stays on your device, with one exception: if you enter a match against other players (VS PLAYERS, § 2.4) while wearing a look or crown style that you bought, the App sends the purchase token for that item to our Online server, which asks Google Play whether the purchase is genuine, so that nobody can wear a paid look they do not own. Our server keeps only a one-way scrambled form (a hash) of the token, together with the item's catalog name and your anonymous game account (§ 2.8); the hash cannot be turned back into the token. Google receives the token and the item's name, both of which it issued in the first place.

From version 1.2.0, if "Share anonymous play stats" is on, the App also tells us which item you bought (its catalog name, such as a shard pack) and whether it was your first purchase, as part of the anonymous statistics in § 2.7. It never sends the purchase token, the order number, the price you paid or anything about your payment.

For purchase history, refunds, subscriptions and payment disputes, Google is the controller — see § 6 and § 11.

2.4 Game modes, Afterimage Online and your display name

Constellations and Hero mode run entirely on your device. Endless, Arcade and Waves also run on your device, and when a run ends its score is posted to the world rankings (§ 2.8).

Afterimage Online has two ways to play:

  • VS BOTS runs entirely on your device against computer-controlled opponents. Nothing about the match is sent anywhere.
  • VS PLAYERS is played live with other people on a server we run on Cloudflare. Rooms are topped up with computer-controlled opponents, which are always labelled BOT. While you are in a match the App sends your steering and dash inputs, your display name, and the look and crown style you are wearing; the server sends your unit's position, size, level and kills to the other players in the room, and theirs to you. Nothing of the live match is kept once it ends, except the record of your run: the name you played under, the level you reached, your kills, how long you stayed alive, how long you held the crown, and when it ended. Your best runs appear on the Online rankings.

Opening the Afterimage Online screen asks our server whether Online is available and sets up your anonymous game account (§ 2.8), whichever way you then play.

Your display name. In Afterimage Online you may type a display name (letters and numbers). It is sent to our server, shown to other players over your unit in a match, and shown beside your scores on the world rankings together with your tag (§ 2.8). If you leave it empty you appear as "Runner" on the rankings and under a generated name in a match. Names are checked against a word filter on your device and again on the server. Please do not enter your real name, an email address or any other personal detail — the App has no need for it. The App has no chat and no way to send a message to another player.

Hero and Arcade mode progress, such as levels, skill points and stash contents, is part of the on-device save described next.

2.5 On-device game save, and Android Backup

The App stores the following locally on your device, using the Android app storage sandbox:

  • your best times and your local top-50 score history;
  • lifetime statistics (runs played, total time survived, shards earned, near misses, distance travelled and similar gameplay counters);
  • unlocked and equipped cosmetics, achievement progress, daily streak state, Hero mode progress and Arcade mode progress;
  • your settings (audio, haptics, joystick size and position, screen shake, colourblind mode, high contrast, quality level);
  • your Afterimage Online display name and an optional local display name for the on-device leaderboard;
  • your anonymous game account's number and its key (§ 2.8).

We never upload this save. The parts of it that do reach our servers are the ones described in § 2.4, § 2.7 and § 2.8. This data is removed when you uninstall the App, or clear its storage from Android Settings → Apps → Afterimage.io → Storage → Clear storage.

Google backup. The App allows Android's automatic backup. If it is turned on for your device, Android copies the App's saved data (its preferences, which hold the save and the settings above) to your own Google account and can restore it when you reinstall the App or set up a new phone (your anonymous game account and its tag come back with it). That backup belongs to you and is governed by Google's terms; we have no access to it. You can turn it off in Android Settings → Google → Backup. It can also be transferred directly to a new device during device-to-device setup.

2.6 Data Google collects as the platform

Independently of the App, Google Play collects crash reports, ANRs and performance metrics from Android devices and shows them to us in aggregate, de-identified form via Android vitals. We cannot use that data to identify you. This is Google's platform-level processing under its own privacy policy.

2.7 Anonymous play statistics

We use anonymous play statistics for two things: to improve the App's performance (finding where it stutters, crashes out of a run, or loses players), and to plan future improvements (finding which parts of the game are confusing, too hard or not fun). The App sends us: when a session starts and how long it lasts; for each run, the mode, how long it lasted, what ended it and the score; whether you then chose to play again or go back to the menu; how far you got through the first lesson and which tips appeared; how long it took to start steering; which ads were shown and whether an optional rewarded ad was watched; your sound, joystick, orientation and tutorial settings when they change; and the App version. From version 1.2.0 it also sends how many shards and coins you earned and from what (a run, a daily reward, an ad...), what you spent them on, what you unlocked, your hero level and progress, when you open the store or start a purchase, and which item you bought (§ 2.3). Versions 1.2.2 to 1.2.8 added, one at a time: whether the App was installed from Google Play; whether Play Games sign-in worked and whether you were shown the sign-in prompt; how many scores were posted to Play Games; and, when a purchase could not start, the reason, the error code Google Play gave and the two-letter country of your Google Play account (for example "US"), so that we can find out why a store fails in some places.

These records carry a random number that the App generates on your phone. It is not your advertising identifier, your Google or Play Games account, or anything else that identifies you or your device, and we do not collect your name or anything you type. Your IP address, and the device model and Android version that your phone's web component names in every request it makes, necessarily reach our hosting provider, Cloudflare, when the App sends the data; we do not record or store either.

This is on by default, except in the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom, where it starts off and is sent only if you turn it on. You can change it at any time in Settings > Privacy > "Share anonymous play stats". Turning it off deletes the random number and any statistics not yet sent from your phone, and nothing further is collected. Because the statistics are not linked to you, we cannot look up or separate the records already sent from a particular phone.

2.8 World rankings and your anonymous game account

The world rankings inside the App (Endless time and Style, Arcade, each Waves layer, the Waves Journey, and Afterimage Online) are kept on a server we run on Cloudflare. This is separate from the anonymous statistics in § 2.7 and is not controlled by that setting.

The anonymous game account. The first time the App needs one — when you finish an Endless, Arcade or Waves run with a score while connected, or when you open Afterimage Online — it asks our server for an account. The server makes up a random account number, a secret key and a random six-character tag (for example #7KQ2MP). There is no sign-up, no email and no password, and the account is not linked to your Google account, your Play Games profile, your advertising ID or any identifier of your device. The number and key are saved in the App's storage on your phone (§ 2.5); our server stores the key only in scrambled (hashed) form.

What is sent and kept. For each ranked run the App sends: which board, the score, how long the run lasted, when it ended, the App version, and a few counters from the run (such as near misses, boosts, Hunters stunned or beaten, Replicas beaten and layers cleared) that let us check the score is possible. The server keeps your best score on each board and your best of each day. The run counters are kept only when a score looks unusual and is set aside for a person to review. The first time rankings run on your phone, the best scores already saved on it are posted once. A score made offline is posted when the connection returns, if that is within three days.

What other players can see. Your display name (or "Runner" if you chose none), your tag, your score and rank on each board, and a badge if you were the first in the world to finish a challenge. Never your account number or key.

Fair play. The server refuses a score automatically only when it is impossible or more than three days old. Anything else that looks unusual stays on the rankings and is flagged for a person to review; we may remove a score or hide an account from the rankings for cheating or for an abusive name.

There is no switch for this. Ranked scores are part of how Endless, Arcade, Waves and VS PLAYERS work, and no setting stops them being posted. Constellations, Hero mode and VS BOTS never post anything, and nothing is posted while your phone is offline. You can ask us to delete your account and everything our server holds for it at any time (§ 10).

Your IP address reaches Cloudflare with every request, as it does with any internet service. Our server uses the broad region the request came from (the continent) to pick the nearest game server and does not store it. When a new account is created, the server keeps a scrambled (hashed) form of the IP address for a short time, normally about a day, only to stop one address creating accounts in bulk. We keep no other record of IP addresses and do not log them.

Afterimage.io is not directed to children under 13. Its store listing, artwork and copy are deliberately abstract and are not designed to appeal to children.

If you believe a child has provided personal information through the App, contact us at svenvike@gmail.com and we will act on it promptly — in practice that means deleting that player's anonymous game account, name and scores from our rankings server (send us its tag, § 10), and telling you how to clear the App's local storage and how to delete any Play Games profile data. 9. Your rights and choices

9.1 Everyone

  • Play without signing in. Skip Play Games sign-in and nothing is sent to Google Play Games. (The App's own world rankings do not use Play Games; see § 2.8.)
  • Play offline. With no network connection, no ads load and nothing is transmitted. The game is fully playable in this state. (A ranked score made offline is posted when the connection returns, if that is within three days.)
  • Stay nameless. Leave the Afterimage Online name empty and the rankings show you as "Runner" with your tag.
  • Have your rankings data deleted. Send us your tag and we delete the anonymous game account, name, scores and run records our server holds (§ 10).
  • Reset all local data by clearing the App's storage in Android Settings → Apps → Afterimage.io → Storage, or by uninstalling.
  • Reset or delete your advertising ID in Android Settings → Google → Ads ("Reset advertising ID" / "Delete advertising ID"). Deleting it stops the App and its ad partner from receiving a persistent advertising identifier.
  • Remove ads entirely by purchasing the premium unlock. This stops both rewarded and interstitial ads being requested at all, and with them the associated data collection.

9.2 United Kingdom and European Economic Area (UK GDPR / EU GDPR)

Before any ad is requested from a user in the UK or EEA, the App presents Google's User Messaging Platform, a Google-certified consent management platform that collects consent under the EU user consent policy and the IAB Transparency and Consent Framework. Where that form applies to you, you can revisit and change your choice at any time from Settings → Privacy options inside the App.

You have the right to: access your data; rectify inaccurate data; erase data ("right to be forgotten"); restrict or object to processing, including objecting to processing based on legitimate interests; data portability; and to withdraw consent at any time without affecting processing already carried out.

Exercise them by emailing svenvike@gmail.com. We respond within one month. For the world rankings and Afterimage Online, tell us your tag (shown under your name on the Afterimage Online screen and beside your name on the rankings): with it we can show you, correct or delete everything our server holds for that account. For advertising, purchases and Play Games we will have to direct you to Google, because that data is held by Google and not by us — we will tell you plainly which of your requests we can act on and which must go to Google, and we will give you the link.

You may also complain to your national supervisory authority. In the UK that is the Information Commissioner's Office, https://ico.org.uk. In the EEA, the list is at https://edpb.europa.eu/about-edpb/about-edpb/members_en.

11. Security

The App transmits everything that leaves the device over encrypted HTTPS/TLS connections, using the Google SDKs' own transport and, for the world rankings, Afterimage Online and anonymous play statistics, HTTPS and secure WebSockets only. Local save data is held in the Android application sandbox, which the operating system isolates from other apps. Our servers are the world rankings and Online server (§ 2.8) and the anonymous play statistics store (§ 2.7). Neither holds email addresses, passwords, payment details, advertising identifiers or game saves; the rankings server stores account keys and purchase tokens only in hashed form; and reports and administrative functions are reachable only with an administrator key.

No method of transmission or storage is completely secure. If we ever become aware of a breach affecting personal data for which we are the controller, we will notify the relevant supervisory authority and affected users as required by law.
