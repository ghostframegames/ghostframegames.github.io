Privacy Policy — Afterimage.io

Effective date: October 15, 2026
Last updated: October 30, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what information the mobile game Afterimage.io (package name com.afterimage.game, the "App") collects, why, who it is shared with, and what choices you have.

The App is published by Alec Vautherot, an individual developer trading as Ghostframe Games, of United States ("we", "us", "our"). For the purposes of the UK GDPR and EU GDPR we are the data controller for the processing described here, except where this policy states that Google acts as an independent controller.

If you do not agree with this policy, please do not install or use the App.

1. Summary
Do we require an account? No. The App is fully playable with no sign-in and with no internet connection.
Is the App free? Yes, with ads. It shows ads unless you buy the premium unlock, which removes them.
Do we run our own servers? One, for anonymous play statistics only. If "Share anonymous play stats" is on (it is on by default, except in the EEA and the UK, where it starts off), the App sends a small record of how the game is played to a server we run on Cloudflare. It carries a random number made by the App, never your name or any account or advertising identifier. You can turn it off in Settings > Privacy. We run no account system and store no game saves.
Where does your progress live? On your device. Best times, shards, unlocked cosmetics, achievements, hero and arcade progress, purchases and settings are stored in local app storage. Android may also back that storage up to your own Google account (§ 2.5).
What leaves the device? Advertising identifier, approximate location, ad interaction data and diagnostics (to Google AdMob); your Play Games display name, player ID and submitted scores only if you sign in to Play Games; purchase transactions processed by Google Play Billing; and, if "Share anonymous play stats" is on, anonymous play statistics (§ 2.7; on by default, except in the EEA and the UK, where it starts off).
Do we sell your data? No. We never sell personal information for money. See § 9 for how US state privacy laws treat personalised advertising as "sharing".
Is the App for children? No. It is not directed to children under 13 and is rated for ages 13 and over. See § 8.

2. Information the App collects

2.1 Advertising: AdMob (rewarded and interstitial ads)

The App is funded by advertising supplied by Google AdMob. There are two kinds:

  • Rewarded ads. You choose whether to watch one; they are never forced. Watching one multiplies the shards earned from a run.
  • Interstitial ads. A full-screen ad that can appear between runs, on a schedule limited by number of runs and by time: not in your first session or first runs, and at most once per few minutes of play. It never appears during a run or on launch.

Buying the premium unlock turns both off. With it, the App stops requesting ads.

To serve, cap, measure and bill for ads, the Google Mobile Ads SDK collects and transmits to Google:

  • your device's Android Advertising ID (AAID) — a resettable identifier not tied to your name;
  • approximate location, derived from your IP address at the country/region level. The App does not request GPS or precise location, and no location permission appears in its manifest;
  • app interactions: which ad was requested, whether it was shown, whether it was watched to completion, whether it was clicked, and timestamps;
  • diagnostics: crash and performance data from the ad SDK, and technical data about the device and connection — device model, operating system version, app version, language, network type and screen size.

The legal basis for personalised advertising in the UK and EEA is your consent, collected through Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP), a Google-certified consent management platform, the first time an ad is available. Where you decline, the SDK requests non-personalised advertising, which still uses the advertising ID for frequency capping, fraud prevention and billing. In regions where the consent form is required you can review or change your choice at any time (§ 9.2).

Google acts as an independent controller for the advertising data it receives. Its own policy governs what it does with it: see § 6.

2.2 Google Play Games Services (optional) and leaderboards

The App integrates Google Play Games Services so scores can appear on public leaderboards and achievements can be recorded against your Play Games profile. This is entirely optional — the App is fully functional signed out, and falls back to leaderboards kept on your own device.

If you are signed in to Play Games, the following is processed:

  • your Play Games player ID and Play Games display name (a gamertag you chose in the Play Games app — not necessarily your real name);
  • your avatar image, as displayed by Google's own leaderboard interface;
  • the scores you set, submitted to the App's four leaderboards: Best Survival Time and Weekly Best Time (Classic mode), Style score and Arcade score. When you sign in, your saved bests on these boards are submitted again so they appear;
  • achievement unlock events, once the App reports achievements to Play Games. (This version defines 42 achievements locally and does not yet send them; if that changes, this is what would be shared.)

Leaderboards are public within Play Games: other players can see your display name, avatar and submitted scores. We do not receive, store or transmit your Google account email address, and we never see your Google password.

You control your Play Games visibility and can delete your Play Games profile and its data from the Google Play Games app itself — see § 10.

2.3 Purchases

Optional in-app purchases (shard packs, a premium unlock that removes ads, cosmetic and character packs) are processed entirely by Google Play Billing. Google, not us, handles your payment method, billing address, tax and receipts. We never see or receive your card details.

The App receives a purchase token from Google confirming a transaction succeeded. That token is stored in local app storage on your device, so the App knows what you own and never grants the same purchase twice. It is not transmitted to us or to any third party other than Google Play, and it is not linked to any profile we hold. We do not operate server-side receipt validation.

For purchase history, refunds, subscriptions and payment disputes, Google is the controller — see § 6 and § 11.

2.4 Game modes and the Afterimage Online display name

The App's modes (Constellations, Endless survival, Hero mode, Arcade mode and Afterimage Online) all run entirely on your device; the only gameplay information that leaves it is the anonymous statistics in § 2.7. In particular, Afterimage Online is currently played against computer-controlled bots; there is no matchmaking, no other human player, and no network traffic for it.

In Afterimage Online you may type a display name. It is shown over your own unit during a match and is checked against a word filter on the device. It is stored on the device only and is not sent to us, to other players or to any server. Please do not enter your real name, an email address or any other personal detail — the App has no need for it. (If a future version adds real-player matches, this policy will be updated and the name will be described here before that version ships.)

Hero and Arcade mode progress, such as levels, skill points, stash contents and best scores, is part of the on-device save described next.

2.5 On-device game save, and Android Backup

The App stores the following locally on your device, using the Android app storage sandbox:

  • your best times and your local top-50 score history;
  • lifetime statistics (runs played, total time survived, shards earned, near misses, distance travelled and similar gameplay counters);
  • unlocked and equipped cosmetics, achievement progress, daily streak state, Hero mode progress and Arcade mode progress;
  • your settings (audio, haptics, joystick size and position, screen shake, colourblind mode, high contrast, quality level);
  • your Afterimage Online display name and an optional local display name for the on-device leaderboard.

We never upload this save ourselves. This data is removed when you uninstall the App, or clear its storage from Android Settings → Apps → Afterimage.io → Storage → Clear storage.

Google backup. The App allows Android's automatic backup. If it is turned on for your device, Android copies the App's saved data (its preferences, which hold the save and the settings above) to your own Google account and can restore it when you reinstall the App or set up a new phone. That backup belongs to you and is governed by Google's terms; we have no access to it. You can turn it off in Android Settings → Google → Backup. It can also be transferred directly to a new device during device-to-device setup.

2.6 Data Google collects as the platform

Independently of the App, Google Play collects crash reports, ANRs and performance metrics from Android devices and shows them to us in aggregate, de-identified form via Android vitals. We cannot use that data to identify you. This is Google's platform-level processing under its own privacy policy.

2.7 Anonymous play statistics

To find out which parts of the game are confusing, too hard or not fun, the App sends us anonymous play statistics: when a session starts and how long it lasts; for each run, the mode, how long it lasted, what ended it and the score; whether you then chose to play again or go back to the menu; how far you got through the first lesson and which tips appeared; how long it took to start steering; which ads were shown and whether an optional rewarded ad was watched; your sound, joystick, orientation and tutorial settings when they change; and the App version.

These records carry a random number that the App generates on your phone. It is not your advertising identifier, your Google or Play Games account, or anything else that identifies you or your device, and we do not collect your name or anything you type. Your IP address, and the device model and Android version that your phone's web component names in every request it makes, necessarily reach our hosting provider, Cloudflare, when the App sends the data; we do not record or store either.

This is on by default, except in the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom, where it starts off and is sent only if you turn it on. You can change it at any time in Settings > Privacy > "Share anonymous play stats". Turning it off deletes the random number and any statistics not yet sent from your phone, and nothing further is collected. Because the statistics are not linked to you, we cannot look up or separate the records already sent from a particular phone.

3. Information the App does not collect

To be explicit, the App does not collect, request, or hold:

  • your name, email address, phone number or postal address;
  • your Google account email address or password;
  • your contacts, calendar, photos, videos, files, SMS or call logs;
  • precise or GPS location;
  • microphone or camera input;
  • health, fitness, biometric or financial account data;
  • browsing history outside the App;
  • any user-generated content that reaches another person — the App has no chat, no messaging and no way to transmit content to another player.

9. Your rights and choices

9.1 Everyone

  • Play without signing in. Skip Play Games sign-in and no leaderboard or achievement data leaves your device.
  • Play offline. With no network connection, no ads load and nothing is transmitted at all. The game is fully playable in this state.
  • Reset all local data by clearing the App's storage in Android Settings → Apps → Afterimage.io → Storage, or by uninstalling.
  • Reset or delete your advertising ID in Android Settings → Google → Ads ("Reset advertising ID" / "Delete advertising ID"). Deleting it stops the App and its ad partner from receiving a persistent advertising identifier.
  • Remove ads entirely by purchasing the premium unlock. This stops both rewarded and interstitial ads being requested at all, and with them the associated data collection.

9.2 United Kingdom and European Economic Area (UK GDPR / EU GDPR)

Before any ad is requested from a user in the UK or EEA, the App presents Google's User Messaging Platform, a Google-certified consent management platform that collects consent under the EU user consent policy and the IAB Transparency and Consent Framework. Where that form applies to you, you can revisit and change your choice at any time from Settings → Privacy options inside the App.

You have the right to: access your data; rectify inaccurate data; erase data ("right to be forgotten"); restrict or object to processing, including objecting to processing based on legitimate interests; data portability; and to withdraw consent at any time without affecting processing already carried out.

Exercise them by emailing svenvike@gmail.com. We respond within one month. In most cases we will have to direct you to Google, because the data in question is held by Google and not by us — we will tell you plainly which of your requests we can act on and which must go to Google, and we will give you the link.

You may also complain to your national supervisory authority. In the UK that is the Information Commissioner's Office, https://ico.org.uk. In the EEA, the list is at https://edpb.europa.eu/about-edpb/about-edpb/members_en.

11. Security

The App transmits everything that leaves the device over encrypted HTTPS/TLS connections, using the Google SDKs' own transport and, for anonymous play statistics, HTTPS only. Local save data is held in the Android application sandbox, which the operating system isolates from other apps. Our only server is the anonymous play statistics store (§ 2.7), which holds no names, accounts, advertising identifiers or game saves, and whose reports are reachable only with an administrator key.

No method of transmission or storage is completely secure. If we ever become aware of a breach affecting personal data for which we are the controller, we will notify the relevant supervisory authority and affected users as required by law.
