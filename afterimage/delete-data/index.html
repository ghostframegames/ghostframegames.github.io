Delete your Afterimage.io data

Afterimage.io (by Ghostframe Games) has no sign-up. This page explains what exists and how to have it removed.

1. World rankings and Afterimage Online (on our server)

The game sets up an anonymous game account for the world rankings: a random number, a six-character tag, the display name you typed (if any), your ranked scores and your Online run records.

  1. Find your tag: the six characters after the # under your name on the Afterimage Online screen, or beside your name on the Rankings.
  2. Email svenvike@gmail.com with the subject "Delete my Afterimage.io data" and your tag.
  3. We delete the account, its tag, name, scores, run records and purchase checks within 30 days, and reply to confirm.

Nothing is kept afterwards. If you go on playing Endless, Arcade, Waves or Afterimage Online, the game sets up a new anonymous account with a new tag.

2. Your save (on your phone)

Progress, settings and purchase records are stored on your device. Remove them in Android Settings → Apps → Afterimage.io → Storage → Clear storage, or uninstall the game. If Android backup is on, also delete the backup in Settings → Google → Backup.

3. Anonymous play statistics

Turn off Settings → Privacy → Share anonymous play stats in the game. That deletes the random number on your phone. Statistics already sent carry no name or account, so we cannot pick out one phone's records; they are deleted automatically after 13 months.

4. Data held by Google

Play Games scores and achievements: Google Play Games app → Settings → Delete Play Games account and data. Advertising ID: Android Settings → Google → Ads. Purchases: your Google Play account.
