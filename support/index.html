Support

Questions, bug reports and requests for Afterimage.io: email svenvike@gmail.com.

What to include

  • Your device model
  • Your Android version
  • What happened, and what you expected to happen

Restore purchases

If you bought Premium or a shard pack and it is missing, open the in-game Store and tap Restore purchases. Purchases are tied to your Google Play account.

Privacy options

Where your region requires it, you can change your ad consent choice in the game under Settings → Privacy options. The full details are in the Privacy Policy.

Request data deletion

Email svenvike@gmail.com and we will act on your request. Save data lives only on your device, so you can also delete it yourself by uninstalling the game or clearing its app data in Android Settings.
